About Us

Nearly two decades of protecting Malaysia's structures.

Founded in 2007, Kencana Painting provides professional surface preparation and protective coating services for industrial and commercial projects.

Our team brings expertise in abrasive blasting, intumescent painting, and anti-corrosion systems, ensuring structures remain safe and compliant.

6 Core service lines
Services

What we do

From bare-metal preparation to the final protective layer, every service is delivered to spec.

Blasting

Abrasive blasting that strips corrosion, coatings and contaminants back to a clean, paint-ready substrate.

Painting

Industrial coating application for structural steel, tanks and equipment, matched to spec and environment.

Epoxy Coating

High-build epoxy systems for chemical resistance and long-term protection in demanding environments.

Cathodic Protection

Sacrificial anode and impressed current systems that stop corrosion before it starts.

Fire Proofing

Intumescent and passive fire protection coatings that maintain structural fire resistance.

Floor Coating

Seamless resin flooring systems — durable, chemical-resistant and easy to maintain.
