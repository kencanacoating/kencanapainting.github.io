WHAT WE DO

Built for corrosion, heat, and wear

Every job starts with the right surface prep — that's where sandblasting comes in — followed by a coating system matched to what the surface actually has to survive.

SVC / 01

Sandblasting

Abrasive blasting to strip rust, old paint, mill scale, and contaminants down to bare, paint-ready metal.

  • Steel structures & tanks
  • Machinery & equipment
  • Pipes & fittings
SVC / 02

Rust-Resistant Painting

Anti-corrosive primer and top-coat systems applied after blasting, built to hold up in humid and coastal conditions.

  • Epoxy / zinc-rich primers
  • Multi-coat systems
  • Indoor & outdoor structures
SVC / 03

Fire-Resistant Painting

Intumescent and heat-resistant coatings for structural steel and equipment exposed to high temperatures or fire risk.

  • Structural steel fireproofing
  • High-temp equipment coating
  • Compliance-driven applications

ABOUT KENCANA PAINTING

19+ years of straightforward, on-schedule work

Kencana Painting is a sandblasting and protective coatings contractor based in Kuala Lumpur, serving clients across Malaysia since 2007. We work directly with facility managers, contractors, and property owners who need corrosion and fire protection done properly the first time.

Every job is quoted after a site visit, so pricing reflects the actual surface condition and access — not a guess. Our work has been trusted by major names including Petronas and Sime Darby.
